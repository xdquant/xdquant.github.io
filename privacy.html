Xdquant（以下简称"我们"）非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明我们在你使用 Xdquant App（以下简称"本 App"）时收集哪些信息、如何使用和保护这些信息，以及你享有的权利。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 账户信息：注册和登录时提供的邮箱地址、密码，以及你自行设置的昵称和头像。
  • 交易所 API 信息：你主动添加的交易所 API Key 和 Secret。这些信息会上传到我们的服务器，用于代你查询账户资产，以及执行你在 App 内发起的操作。
  • 使用偏好：自选列表、价格提醒、语言设置等，用于在不同设备间同步你的设置。
  • 订阅状态：你通过 Apple 购买 Xdquant Pro 后，我们会收到订阅状态和有效期。付款由 Apple 处理，我们不会获取你的银行卡或支付信息。

本 App 不接入任何第三方统计、广告或崩溃分析 SDK，不会跨 App 或网站追踪你，也不会收集你的通讯录、照片（你主动上传的头像除外）或精确位置。

2. 我们如何使用信息

  • 创建和维护你的账户，验证你的身份；
  • 通过你授权的 API 读取交易所账户数据，并执行你发起的操作；
  • 提供行情、价格提醒、AI 分析、回测等功能；
  • 管理你的订阅权益；
  • 回复你的咨询，发送与账户安全相关的必要通知。

我们不会出售你的个人信息，也不会将其用于广告。

3. API Key 安全

  • API Key 和 Secret 通过加密连接（HTTPS）传输，在服务器上加密存储，只用于为你提供服务。
  • 强烈建议你在交易所创建 API 时关闭提现权限，并尽量设置 IP 白名单。
  • 你可以随时在 App 内删除已添加的 API，也可以在交易所后台直接撤销。

4. 信息共享

只有以下情况，我们才会共享你的信息：

  • 根据你的指令，通过你的 API 与对应交易所通信；
  • 为提供服务所必需的基础设施服务商（如云服务器），这些服务商须履行保密义务；
  • 法律法规要求，或司法、行政机关依法提出要求。

5. 数据保存与删除

在你的账户存续期间，我们保存上述信息。你可以在 App 内注销账户，或发邮件给我们申请删除。账户注销后，我们会在 30 天内删除你的账户信息和 API 信息，法律要求保留的除外。

6. 你的权利

你有权查看、更正、导出或删除你的个人信息，也可以撤回授权。如需行使这些权利，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会在 15 个工作日内回复。

7. 未成年人

本 App 面向成年用户，不针对未满 18 岁的未成年人提供服务。

8. 政策更新

本政策如有更新，我们会在本页面公布，并更新生效日期。重大变更会在 App 内通知你。

Xdquant ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Xdquant app (the "App"), how we use and protect it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: the email address and password you use to sign up and sign in, plus the nickname and avatar you choose to set.
  • Exchange API credentials: the exchange API Key and Secret you choose to add. They are uploaded to our servers so we can retrieve your account balances and perform actions you initiate in the App.
  • Preferences: watchlists, price alerts and language settings, used to sync your settings across devices.
  • Subscription status: when you buy Xdquant Pro through Apple, we receive your subscription status and expiry date. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your card or payment details.

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not collect your contacts, photos (other than an avatar you upload) or precise location.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account and verify your identity;
  • To read your exchange account data and perform the actions you initiate through the API you authorize;
  • To provide market data, price alerts, AI analysis, backtesting and other features;
  • To manage your subscription benefits;
  • To respond to your inquiries and send necessary account security notices.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. API Key Security

  • API Keys and Secrets are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), stored encrypted on our servers, and used only to provide the service to you.
  • We strongly recommend that you disable withdrawal permissions when creating an API key and enable an IP whitelist where possible.
  • You can remove an API key in the App at any time, or revoke it directly on the exchange.

4. Sharing

We share your information only:

  • With the relevant exchange, via your API, when you instruct us to;
  • With infrastructure providers (e.g. cloud hosting) that are required to provide the service and are bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • When required by law or by a lawful request from a court or government authority.

5. Retention and Deletion

We keep the information above while your account is active. You can delete your account in the App or email us to request deletion. After deletion, we remove your account and API information within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your Rights

You may access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. We will reply within 15 business days.

7. Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

We will post any updates to this policy on this page and update the effective date. We will notify you in the App of any material changes.
