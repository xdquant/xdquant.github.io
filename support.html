常见问题

如何连接交易所账户？
在交易所后台创建 API Key，然后在 App 中进入「Assets → Add API」填入即可。为了安全，建议关闭提现权限，并设置 IP 白名单。

如何删除已添加的 API？
进入「Assets → Manage」即可删除，也可以直接在交易所后台撤销该 API。

如何管理或取消 Xdquant Pro 订阅？
打开 iPhone「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」，选择 Xdquant Pro 即可管理或取消。请在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时关闭自动续订。

换了手机，订阅怎么恢复？
用同一个 Apple ID 登录后，在订阅页点击「恢复购买」即可。

如何申请退款？
订阅款项由 Apple 收取，请前往 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。

如何注销账户？
在 App 内的账户设置中选择注销账户，或发邮件给我们。

FAQ

How do I connect my exchange account?
Create an API key on your exchange, then go to "Assets → Add API" in the App and enter it. For security, please disable withdrawal permissions and enable an IP whitelist.

How do I remove an API key?
Go to "Assets → Manage" to delete it, or revoke it directly on the exchange.

How do I manage or cancel Xdquant Pro?
On your iPhone, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and select Xdquant Pro. Turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

I got a new phone. How do I restore my subscription?
Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen.

How do I request a refund?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Please request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?
Choose Delete Account in the App's account settings, or email us.
