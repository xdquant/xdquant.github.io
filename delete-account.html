删除账号 / Delete Your Account

Xdquant · XDTech Inc.

中文English

方式一：在 App 内删除

打开 Xdquant，进入 Assets → Manage → Delete Account，按提示确认即可。

方式二：发邮件申请

如果无法登录 App，请用注册时的邮箱发送邮件至 xdquant@gmail.com，标题写"删除账号"，并在正文中注明你的账号邮箱。我们会在 7 个工作日内处理，并邮件回复确认。

会删除哪些数据

  • 账户信息：邮箱、昵称、头像
  • 已添加的交易所 API Key 和 Secret
  • 自选列表、价格提醒等偏好设置

以上数据会在账号删除后 30 天内从我们的服务器永久删除，法律法规要求保留的除外。

注意：删除账号不会自动取消订阅。请先在 Google Play 或 App Store 的订阅管理中取消 Xdquant Pro，以免继续扣费。删除账号不影响你在交易所的资产。

Option 1: Delete in the app

Open Xdquant, go to Assets → Manage → Delete Account, and confirm.

Option 2: Request by email

If you cannot sign in, email xdquant@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account" and your account email in the message. We will process your request within 7 business days and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

  • Account information: email, nickname, avatar
  • Exchange API Keys and Secrets you added
  • Preferences such as watchlists and price alerts

This data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days after account deletion, unless we are required by law to keep it.

Note: Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Please cancel Xdquant Pro in Google Play or App Store subscription settings first to avoid further charges. Deleting your account does not affect your assets on any exchange.
